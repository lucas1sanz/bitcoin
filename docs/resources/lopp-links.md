# Jameson Lopp's Bitcoin Resources

Jameson Lopp (CTO of Casa, long-time Bitcoin security researcher) maintains one of the most comprehensive curated lists of Bitcoin resources. This page highlights the sections most relevant to self-custody security.

## About Jameson Lopp

Lopp is known for:
- Operating a Bitcoin node since the early days
- Publishing research on physical Bitcoin security (safe tests, seed backup tests)
- Writing extensively on self-custody, node operation, and privacy
- His famous home security after a swatting incident targeting Bitcoin holders

His resource list at **lopp.net** covers hundreds of links across every Bitcoin topic.

## Lopp's Physical Security Research

Lopp has publicly documented his fire and water tests of seed backup products, ranking plates by durability. His methodology:

1. Place steel plates in a 1500°F furnace for 30 minutes
2. Run over with vehicle (crush test)
3. Submerge in saltwater for 24 hours

Results are published on his GitHub and referenced by most seed backup vendors.
